What We Do

Programs that make a difference

Each program is designed to address a specific gap in education access across rural Indonesia.

📚

Reading Circles

Small group literacy sessions held three times a week in community spaces, led by trained local facilitators.

Ages 6–12
💻

Digital Literacy

Tablet-based learning labs that teach basic computing, internet safety, and digital skills to older students.

Ages 13–18
👩‍🏫

Teacher Training

Monthly workshops that equip local teachers with modern pedagogy, inclusive methods, and mental health tools.

Educators
🌾

Nature School

Outdoor learning programs connecting science education to local agriculture, ecosystems, and environmental care.

All ages
👩‍👧

Parent Engagement

Workshops for parents to support learning at home, understand child development, and reduce dropout rates.

Families
🎓

Scholarship Fund

Financial support for high-achieving students from low-income families to continue to secondary and higher education.

Merit-based
